# Research report — field audit

Every number the research report (`/api/analysis/<TICKER>/report`) renders, where it comes from, when it is true, which price it uses, how it is computed, and what checks it before it is shown.

**Rule:** a blank with a visible warning is acceptable; a wrong number is not. Any field that fails a check renders `—` with a ⚠ chip naming the two numbers that disagree.

**Code:** `backend/analysis/price_basis.py` (the price), `backend/analysis/report_view.py` (fields + checks), `backend/analysis/news_relevance.py` (headlines, news-check flags), `backend/api/report.py` (rendering only — it computes nothing).

## The report price

Chosen once per report by `price_basis.build()`:

| Market state (NY clock, NYSE holiday calendar 2025–2027) | Report price | Day move |
|---|---|---|
| Open (9:30–16:00, 13:00 on early-close days) | live regular-session last (broker quote; withheld if older than 10 min or served from a stale cache) | live ÷ prior official close − 1 |
| Pre-market, after hours, weekend, holiday | official close of the last completed regular session (daily bar; the broker's regular-session last print when the bar is not yet published) | that close ÷ the prior official close − 1 |

An extended-hours quote is shown only as its own labeled line ("after hours, 8:00 PM ET Fri"). The word "today" appears only when the session really is today.

## Security kind and field groups

The analysis payload carries `security_kind` (also at `report_view.security_kind`), one of `stock` | `fund` | `bank` | `new_listing`, from `report_view.classify_security()`, and a top-level boolean `is_new_listing`. The facts behind them are in `security` (`is_fund`, `is_bank`, `new_listing`, `is_new_listing`, `why`, `sessions_listed`, `first_trade_date`, `listed_under_a_year`, `public_quarter_filed`, `sic`, `sic_description`, `asset_sub_type`, `industry`). Top-level `listed_under_a_year` (bool, or null when the listing date is unknown) and `sessions_listed` (completed sessions since the first trade) come from `report_view.listing_facts()`: the short daily history when it is the symbol's whole life, else the long history (`history_5y:{SYMBOL}`, cached a day, not refetched on Refresh) when its first bar is more than 30 days after the start asked for; `listed_under_a_year` = fewer than 252 completed sessions. `public_quarter_filed` = a 10-Q / 10-K filed after the first trade (filing list, else the statements' SEC label). The verdict cap (not better than fair) applies to stocks and banks with `listed_under_a_year` true and no public quarter filed; funds have no cap.

**Precedence contract (the frontend and the research prompt code against it):** `security_kind` is `fund` or `bank` when detected; else `new_listing` when the symbol has fewer than 21 completed sessions; else `stock`. `is_new_listing` is `true` whenever there are fewer than 21 completed sessions, **whatever the kind** — a fund or a bank that listed days ago is `security_kind: "fund"` / `"bank"` with `is_new_listing: true`. Every new-listing behavior in the report keys off `is_new_listing` (or the listing dates), never off `security_kind`.

| Kind | Rule (first match wins) | What changes in the report |
|---|---|---|
| `fund` | broker asset type (ETF, mutual fund) or sub-type (Schwab files ETFs as EQUITY with sub-type ETF / CEF / ETN …), else Yahoo `quoteType` ETF / MUTUALFUND, else — only with no sub-type, no quote type and no financial statements — ETF / ETN / Fund / iShares in the name, unless the name also has Corp / Inc / Bancorp / Holdings / Ordinary / Depositary / REIT / Realty ("Northern Trust Corp", "Vornado Realty Trust", "… Ordinary Shares", "American Depositary Shares" are stocks; "Trust" and "Shares" alone are not fund words). Unknown → not a fund | no company-fundamentals rows (`market_cap`, `eps_ttm`, `pe_ttm`, `fcf_ttm`, `p_fcf`, `ev`, `net_cash`, margins, revenue growth); yields relabeled "Distribution yield"; fund rows below; no value lens (`value_lens` = null); the HTML export shows a "Fund" card instead of "Fundamentals" |
| `bank` | SEC SIC code 6021 / 6022 / 6029 / 6035 / 6036 (EDGAR submissions). Only when the SIC code is unknown: Yahoo industry "Banks - …", else net interest income + deposit lines in the XBRL filing (a known non-bank SIC is final) | `ev`, `net_cash`, `p_fcf`, `fcf_ttm`, `gross_margin` (and an empty `operating_margin`) not shown, replaced by one `bank_note` row; `p_tbv` and `roe` added; value lens blanks its FCF / EV / ROIC / cash-paid-for-acquisitions rows; vendor TTM revenue growth withheld |
| `new_listing` | not a fund or bank, and fewer than 21 completed sessions of price history (only when the history returned is shorter than requested, i.e. it is the symbol's whole life) | see `is_new_listing` below |
| `stock` | anything else | — |

New-listing behavior (any kind; keyed off `is_new_listing` or the listing dates): `market_cap` names the share count used and flags a listing under 60 sessions old (or any `is_new_listing` symbol: "newly listed — …"); `net_cash` / `ev` flagged when the balance sheet predates the first trading day (EV withheld); an empty rating feed is "no data", not zero, and says "(listed days ago)".

Every field in `report_view.fields` carries `group`, one of `price` | `fundamentals` | `fund` | `bank` | `valuation` | `rules` | `options` | `context` | `position` | `news` | `research` (the list, in render order, is `report_view.groups`). A UI renders by group; a row absent from `fields` does not apply to this security. For a fund the two yield rows are in group `fund`, for everything else in `fundamentals`.

## Valuation range (payload `valuation_range`)

`value_lens.build_valuation_range()`, wired in `composer._valuation_range()` (contract: `docs/VERDICT_SPEC.md` §6). The research session grades value from it; `grade_hint` (reported figure) and `normalized_grade_hint` (five-year average) are the arithmetic only. Warnings are written for the reader — numbers to one decimal with "x" or "%", no instructions to the session.

```json
 "grade_hint": "cheap" | "fair" | "expensive" | null,
 "normalized": false, "now_normalized": null, "normalized_grade_hint": null,
 "grade": "cheap" | "fair" | "expensive" | null,     // the less favorable of grade_hint and normalized_grade_hint
 "excluded_points": [{"fy_end": "2021-12-31", "value": 1960.25, "price": 300.2}],   // only when a year was left out
 "warnings": ["..."],
 "status": "ok" | "withheld" | "no_data"}
```

| Kind | Measure | Now | Year-end points |
|---|---|---|---|
| stock (and a new listing) | `p_fcf`; `pe` when TTM free cash flow is ≤ 0 or missing; `p_sales` when TTM net income is ≤ 0 too (loss-making), or when `p_fcf` / `pe` has fewer than 3 usable year-ends because some years' figure was ≤ 0 and price to sales has 3 or more (`ev_ebit` is not computed — it would need a balance sheet at every year-end) | the report's `p_fcf` / `pe_ttm`; `p_sales` = report market cap ÷ TTM revenue | close at each of the last five fiscal year-ends ÷ that year's free cash flow (net income for `pe`, revenue for `p_sales`) per diluted share |
| bank | `p_tbv` | the report's `p_tbv` | close ÷ tangible book per diluted share at the year-end (equity − preferred − goodwill & intangibles; a preferred line not filed within a year of the year-end counts as none) |
| fund | `distribution_rate` (%) | the report's `div_yield_trailing` | at the last five **calendar** year-ends: distributions ex-dated in the 365 days to Dec 31 ÷ the Dec 31 close, counted like the rate now (a regular payer's extra ex-date inside 365 days is last year's payment — `trailing_distributions`). Higher = cheaper: cheap ≥ 15% above the median; expensive below the low or under two-thirds of the median |

Inputs: the long history is ~6 years of split-adjusted daily closes with the split and distribution events in the same series (capability `long_history`, Yahoo; cached a day as `history_5y:{SYMBOL}`, not refetched on Refresh, failures back off 15 minutes). Fiscal years come from the statements' `annual` series, which now carries `shares_diluted` (fiscal-year weighted-average diluted count — the SEC candidate tags `WeightedAverageNumberOfDilutedSharesOutstanding` and `WeightedAverageNumberOfShareOutstandingBasicAndDiluted` are combined per period, so a company that moved tags keeps its older years), `shares_filed` (SEC filing date of that count; null from Yahoo) and `tangible_book`. A cached statements copy without those keys is refetched once. The same per-year note for several years is merged into one warning ("FY ended 2021-01-31, 2020-01-31: no price … — left out").

Share counts are put on today's share basis: a count filed before a later stock split is multiplied by that split (SEC filing date); with no filing date (Yahoo) the count closer to today's share count (as filed, or split-adjusted) is taken. A year is **left out**, with its reason in `warnings`, when its figure is ≤ 0 (the multiple is undefined), when no close lies within 10 days before the year-end (price history too short), or when its share count is more than 2× away from today's (an unreconciled split or share change). Fewer than 3 points → `status: "no_data"` (graded against peers by the session). ADR / withheld market cap / statements in another currency → `status: "withheld"` (the session writes `value_grade: paused`); the long history is not fetched then. A year-end multiple more than 5× the median of the other years (a near-zero cash-flow or profit year — MNDY FY2021 1960x) is moved from `points` to `excluded_points`, left out of median / low / high, and named in `warnings` ("FY2021 (ended 2021-12-31) excluded: free cash flow near zero — …"); the 3-point minimum is counted after it. When TTM free cash flow (or net income for `pe`) is more than 2× its fiscal-year average and every one of those years is ≥ 0, `normalized: true`, `now_normalized` = now × TTM ÷ average and `normalized_grade_hint` = the grade on it; `grade_hint` stays the grade on the reported figure, `grade` = the less favorable of the two, and a warning states both ("current free cash flow is 3.2× its five-year average; on that average the measure is 77.0x instead of 23.8x — reported grade cheap, normalized grade fair; the value grade is the less favorable of the two"). A negative year (a turnaround, e.g. TWLO) → no normalization: the average means nothing.

## Rule inputs (payload `rule_inputs`)

`value_lens.build_rule_inputs()`, wired in `composer._rule_inputs()` (contract: `docs/VERDICT_SPEC.md` §3 and §6b). The app's arithmetic for each data-driven soft signal; inputs and hints only — the session writes `soft_signals`, starting each entry with its rule key in brackets (`[ni_over_fcf] …`).

```json
{"kind": "stock" | "bank" | "fund",
 "rules": [{"key": "ni_over_fcf", "label": "...", "values": [{"period": "2026-09-30", ...}, ...],
            "threshold": "adjusted net income > 1.25 × free cash flow in both quarters",
            "fires": true | false | null, "basis": "SEC filed quarters (10-Q filed 2026-10-30) ...",
            "warnings": ["..."], "summary": "2026-09-30 ratio 1.57, 2026-06-30 ratio 1.3"}],
 "as_of": "2026-09-30", "filed": "2026-10-30", "status": "ok" | "no_data", "warnings": []}
```

Quarters: the SEC's own filed quarters (`statements.rule_quarters`, up to 9, newest first: revenue, operating / pretax / net income, tax, free cash flow — only through the cash-flow statement's end — interest expense, D&A, the quarter's diluted weighted-average count (3-month fact; none for a 10-K Q4), and quarter-end receivables, inventory, cash + short-term investments, debt, and for banks non-interest expense, net interest income, non-interest income, deposits). A year-earlier quarter is the one ending 350–380 days before. TTM sums need four consecutive quarters (75–105 days apart).

| Key | Values (per quarter, newest first) | Fires |
|---|---|---|
| `revenue_decline` | `revenue`, `revenue_year_ago`, `growth_pct` | growth < 0 in both of the last two quarters (banks: revenue, else net interest + non-interest income) |
| `op_margin_decline` | `op_margin_pct`, `op_margin_year_ago_pct`, `change_pts` | change < −3 points in both |
| `fcf_negative` | `fcf`, `revenue_growth_pct` | fcf < 0 in both; false ("skipped") when the latest quarter's revenue grows > 25% |
| `receivables_vs_revenue` / `inventory_vs_revenue` | `receivables_growth_pct` / `inventory_growth_pct`, `revenue_growth_pct`, `gap_pts` | gap > 20 points in both; null when the line is not filed |
| `ni_over_fcf` | `net_income`, `net_income_adj`, `fcf`, `ratio` | adjusted net income > 0 and (fcf ≤ 0 or > 1.25 × fcf) in both. Adjusted = net income less the one-time items the report flags: an income-tax benefit ≥ 10% of pretax profit → taxed at 21% instead; non-operating gains ≥ 5% of pretax income → removed after tax (named in `warnings`) |
| `net_debt_ebitda` | `net_debt`, `ebitda_ttm`, `ratio`, `net_debt_year_ago` | net debt > 3 × TTM (operating income + D&A) and net debt higher than a year earlier; net cash → false; EBITDA ≤ 0 → null |
| `interest_coverage` | `operating_income_ttm`, `interest_expense_ttm`, `coverage` | TTM operating income ÷ TTM interest expense < 3; no interest line and zero debt → false; no interest line otherwise → null |
| `dilution` | `shares_diluted`, `shares_diluted_year_ago`, `growth_pct` | > 5% year on year (latest quarter with a 3-month count a year apart); false in the first 252 sessions after a listing |
| `efficiency_ratio` (banks) | `efficiency_pct` | non-interest expense ÷ (net interest income + non-interest income) > 70% in both |
| `deposit_decline` (banks) | `deposits`, `deposits_prior_quarter`, `change_pct` | down > 5% in the latest quarter |
| `npl_rise`, `charge_offs`, `capital_ratio`, `nim_decline`, `cre_concentration` (banks) | — | always null — not in the filed data consistently |
| `distribution_cut` (funds) | `latest`, `average`, `months`, `payments`, `change_pct` | latest < 90% of the average of payments in the 3 months (monthly payers) or 6 months (others) before it; payments more than twice the median are left out of the average (specials) |
| `assets_decline` (funds) | — | null — no history of fund assets |

Banks get `revenue_decline`, `dilution`, `efficiency_ratio`, `deposit_decline` and the null bank rules (the stock-only balance-sheet and cash-flow rules do not apply). Event rules (`guidance_cut`, `dividend_cut`, `exec_exit`, `insider_selling`) are not computed. A rule with a missing or flagged input is `fires: null` with the reason in its `warnings` (the data-quality gate). A cached SEC statements copy without `rule_quarters` is refetched once.

Check (`research_quality.rule_input_flags`): for a v6 note, a `soft_signals` entry whose `[key]` has `fires: false`, or no entry for a key with `fires: true`, raises "soft signal X does not match the app's arithmetic: the app computes it as met / not met (threshold; summary)". `fires: null` is never checked; the check is skipped when `rule_inputs.filed` is later than the note's date.

## Verdict (prompt v6) — `claude_stance` keys and checks

Contract: `docs/VERDICT_SPEC.md`. Parsing (`discussions_svc/parser.py`): `verdict`, `value_grade`, `risk_grade` are enums (a value the app cannot read goes to `research_meta.unreadable` and is flagged); `value_basis`, `verdict_note`, `owner_note` are strings; `hard_flags`, `soft_signals`, `watch_items` are lists (template placeholders dropped). A note with `prompt_version` ≥ 6 (or carrying a verdict key) and no `stance` gets the legacy `stance` column derived — attractive → buy, fair → hold, stretched → trim, avoid → sell, unrated → watch — and `stance_note` from `verdict_note` (`research_meta.stance_derived: true`), so the scorecard and sorting keep working. Notes before v6 are untouched.

`claude_stance` in the analysis payload keeps every earlier key and adds `status` / `status_reason` (the note's OK / STALE / INVALID / SUPERSEDED), `trust`, `is_new_listing`, `listed_under_a_year`, `public_quarter_filed`, `security_kind` (the cap's inputs), and `format`: `"v6"` with `verdict`, `value_grade`, `risk_grade`, `verdict_label` ("Stretched (Expensive · Watch)"), `value_basis`, `verdict_note`, `hard_flags`, `soft_signals`, `watch_items`, `owner_line`, `owner_note`, `expected_verdict`, `expected_risk`; `"earlier"` for a note from before v6. The report field `claude_stance` carries the same keys (`note_status` for the note's status). Watchlist rows (`/api/watchlist/screener`) carry `verdict`, `verdict_label`, `value_grade`, `risk_grade`, `trust`, `owner_line`, `owner_note`, `format`, `listed_under_a_year`, `stance_status` beside the stance columns (the screener applies the cap from cached rows only; the value-grade and soft-signal checks need the analysis payload and are not in the watchlist warnings). Every `GET /api/discussions` row carries `format` ("v6" / "earlier"), `verdict`, `value_grade`, `risk_grade`, `verdict_label` (null for an earlier note).

`owner_line` (§5, derived by the app): ownership from the account (`account_owned`, linked share classes count), else the note's `owned`, else both lines ("If you own it: hold · if not: wait"). `owner_line` is the derived line only; `owner_note` (the user's own rules) is a separate field everywhere (`claude_stance`, Watchlist rows, the report field); the HTML export prints "owner_line — owner_note" once.

Checks (`research_quality.verdict_flags`, `value_grade_flags`), joined to the stance warnings:

| Check | Flag |
|---|---|
| `verdict` vs the rulebook (`expected_verdict`), first match wins: research_status failed and no hard flag → unrated; any hard flag → avoid (even when research failed or value is paused); 3+ soft signals → avoid; value_grade paused / missing → unrated; else baseline (cheap → attractive, fair → fair, expensive → stretched), one step worse with exactly 2 soft signals; cap (`research_quality.capped`: stocks and banks with `listed_under_a_year`, no public quarter filed): nothing better than fair. Watch items never count. Unrated is also accepted when `research_status` is partial and the rulebook does not give avoid | "verdict does not follow the rulebook: expected X from value_grade …, n hard flag(s), n soft signal(s)[, listed under a year] — the note says Y" |
| unrated `verdict_note` prefix: "research incomplete" only when research_status is partial / failed; "value paused" when value_grade is paused | "verdict_note starts \"research incomplete\" but research_status is …" / "unrated with value_grade paused — verdict_note should start \"value paused\" …" |
| `risk_grade` vs the lists (`expected_risk`): hard or 3+ soft → red_flag; 2 soft → watch; 0–1 soft with watch items → watch; else clean | "risk_grade does not match the lists: expected …" |
| `value_grade` vs `valuation_range.grade` (the less favorable of the two hints; only when there is one) | "value_grade X differs from the app's valuation range (grade Y[ (reported A, normalized B)]: …) and value_basis gives no departure reason" — not raised for a one-step difference when `value_basis` has a departure word (normalized, one-time, non-recurring, peer, instead of, rather than, depart, override); a two-step difference (cheap vs expensive) → "value_grade X is two steps from the app's valuation range (…) — a two-step departure is not allowed", always |
| `soft_signals` vs `rule_inputs` (see "Rule inputs") | "soft signal X does not match the app's arithmetic: …" |
| required v6 fields: `verdict`, `value_grade`, `risk_grade`, `value_basis`, `research_status`, `price_used`, `stance_confidence` | "missing required field(s): … — the note does not follow prompt v6" (an unreadable one is listed under "unreadable field(s)") |
| `upgrade_if`, `downgrade_if`, `review_by` for every verdict except unrated | "<verdict> with no conditions — …" |
| a v6 note that also writes a `stance` that does not match its verdict | "the note writes stance … beside verdict …" |

All v5 checks (price used, research status, move cause, review date, superseded, ownership mismatch, triggers met) stay.

**Trust labels** (§9) — `research_quality.trust(status)`: OK → `current` ("Current"), STALE → `out_of_date` ("Out of date"), INVALID → `dont_use` ("Don't use"), SUPERSEDED → `replaced` ("Replaced"); a note not judged (null status) → null. Carried as `trust` beside `status` on `claude_stance` (and its report field), Watchlist rows (beside `stance_status`) and every `GET /api/discussions` row; the HTML export prints the label beside each note's status chip and beside the verdict.

## Statements: sources and freshness

Primary source: **SEC XBRL company facts** (the numbers as filed in the 10-Q/10-K, with form and filing date). Fallback: Yahoo statements. The two are merged **per statement** (income statement, cash flow, balance sheet): for each, the source whose latest *complete* period is newest wins; SEC wins ties. Each field is labeled with its own statement's period and source.

TTM from filings: `TTM = YTD + prior FY − prior-year YTD` (10-Q cash flows are year-to-date only); quarters are the 3-month facts, or YTD differences (Q4 = FY − 9 months).

When the last earnings release postdates the cached copy (or the copy predates the current format), both sources are refetched before any TTM figure is computed. Any statement whose period ends more than 95 days before the release carries "does not include the latest quarter" on every field that depends on it. If the release itself is on EDGAR (8-K item 2.02, `statements_meta.release`: `url` = the 8-K's primary document, i.e. the cover page; `exhibit_url` = its EX-99.1 exhibit — the earnings release itself — found through the filing index, else the first EX-99.* exhibit; absent when the index has none or EDGAR is unreachable), the warning says the results are only in that release until the 10-Q is filed, and the research prompt tells the session to read it.

Balance-sheet notes: SEC debt = long-term debt (current + noncurrent) + short-term borrowings, **excluding leases**; Yahoo "Total Debt" may include leases. Marketable equity securities are reported only from the SEC tag `EquitySecuritiesFvNiCurrent` — Yahoo's investment rows mix categories, so they are not used. Banks: cash falls back to `CashCashEquivalentsAndFederalFundsSold` / `CashAndDueFromBanks` (many banks stopped filing the generic cash tag); tangible-book inputs are `IntangibleAssetsNetIncludingGoodwill` (else `Goodwill` + `IntangibleAssetsNetExcludingGoodwill` / `FiniteLivedIntangibleAssetsNet`) and `PreferredStockValue`. Weighted-average share counts (basic, diluted) of the latest quarter travel as `statements.shares`.

Fiscal-year series (value lens): the SEC series only when the SEC income statement is complete (a revenue tag that gives no TTM gives an unreliable FY series too); otherwise the series of the source the income statement came from (`annual_source`). The revenue CAGR names its fiscal years in the basis; when TTM revenue is 15%+ away from the last fiscal year a warning says the latest quarters are not in the CAGR; outside 0.5–2× the CAGR is withheld (different revenue definitions). `acquisitions_total` is labeled "Cash paid for acquisitions" — the cash-flow line, net of cash acquired; deals paid in stock are not in it; blank for a bank. A fiscal year with no end date is named "latest FY (undated)" / "FY −n (undated)". When market cap is withheld (ADR, share counts far apart) the lens blanks P/FCF, P/FCF avg, P/E avg and EV; for a non-USD filer it also blanks FCF, EV and acquisitions and labels amounts in that currency ("TWD 3.81T"); CAGR, ROIC~ and payout of FCF are kept.

Non-USD reporters (Yahoo `financialCurrency`, carried as `security.financial_currency`): `fcf_ttm`, `net_cash`, `ev`, `eps_ttm`, `pe_ttm`, `p_fcf` and `p_tbv` are withheld with "reported in <currency> — the filing's amounts are not US dollars; not shown"; margins, growth and ROE (ratios of two amounts in the same currency) are shown; amounts quoted in warnings carry the currency code.

Refetch markers: a cached copy found lacking (old format, missing field, predating the last earnings) is force-refreshed at most once per 30 minutes per key (`refresh_attempt:<key>` in the cache), whatever the outcome — a failed refresh no longer loops. A failed EX-99.1 exhibit lookup is cached as `{"exhibit_url": null}` for an hour; a security-profile network error backs off 15 minutes.
